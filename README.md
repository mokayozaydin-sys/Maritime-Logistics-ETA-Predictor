# Maritime-Logistics-ETA-Predictor
## 🚢 Maritime Logistics: The "Control Tower" Project

In the world of logistics, it’s easy to analyze when everything goes right. The real challenge is knowing what happens when a storm hits the Atlantic or a major port becomes congested. This project is a data-driven answer to the question: **"What happens next?"**

## 🌟 Why I Built This
As an Industrial Engineering student and an intern at **Mars Logistics**, I’ve always been fascinated by how uncertainties in the supply chain can cripple operational efficiency. It’s not enough to just say "the ship is on its way." I wanted to simulate **why** it might be delayed and how that ripple effect impacts the entire journey. This dashboard serves as a "Command Center" for maritime operations.

## 🧠 The "Brain": Python Simulation
The dataset was built from scratch using **Python (Pandas & NumPy)**. Instead of using random numbers, I engineered a logical architecture to mirror real-world maritime constraints:

* **Weather Correlation:** I developed an algorithm that assigns disruption probabilities based on weather severity. (e.g., a 5% risk in 'Clear' weather vs. an 80% risk during a 'Storm').
* **Multi-Stage Routing:** Ships don't just go from A to B. I modeled complex routes including major transit hubs like **Tanger Med, Jebel Ali, and Singapore** to reflect global trade flows.
* **5,000 Unique Scenarios:** I stress-tested the logistics network across 5,000 unique shipments to ensure statistical depth and reliability.

## 📊 The "Eyes": Tableau Dashboard
I transformed raw code into a visual intelligence platform designed for operational managers to make split-second decisions:

* **Global Risk Map:** Instant visualization of where congestion is happening and which routes are currently most reliable.
* **Live ETA Predictor:** By clicking on a single shipment, you get a "Digital Ticket" showing its origin, transit hub, and final arrival date—all adjusted for real-time weather impacts.
* **Parametric Simulation:** Users can interact with "Vessel Speed" or "Port Stay Duration" parameters to see how tactical changes affect the overall lead time.

## 🛠️ Technical Stack
* **Language:** Python (The core simulation engine)
* **Visualization:** Tableau Public (Visual intelligence & UI)
* **Domain Logic:** Supply Chain Management, Operational Risk Modeling, Lead Time Variability
